Wiring

Power: one USB-C cable runs the box and charges the battery

USB-C goes into the SuperMini. Its 5 V pin also feeds your charger board's input, so plugging in charges the battery. The battery side joins the 5 V pin through a diode. That way the battery powers the box only when USB is unplugged, and USB never pushes current backwards into the battery. The micro-USB on the charger board still works as a second way to charge.

First, find out which pads are which on your charger board. The battery is already on B+/B−. Then check C+/C− with a multimeter:
• Micro-USB unplugged: 3.7–4.2 V on C+/C− → C+/C− is the battery output. The 5 V input is then the micro-USB's VBUS pin.
• 0 V, but about 5 V when a charger is plugged into the micro-USB → C+/C− is the 5 V input.
Measure the 3 socket pins the same way: one of them is probably the output.
