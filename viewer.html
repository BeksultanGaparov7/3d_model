Find the charger pads

Your board has B+/B− (battery), C+/C−, a micro-USB and a 3-pin socket. Find out which is which with a multimeter:

  • OUT +: with nothing plugged in, the pad that shows 3.7–4.2 V to ground. Usually C+ or a socket pin. If only B+ shows it, use B+.
  • 5 V IN +: the micro-USB's 5 V pin. It's the outer pin of the 5 small pins, and you can solder a wire to its leg on the board. If a C pad shows about 5 V when a charger is plugged into the micro-USB, use that pad instead.
  • GND: B− side ground, usually C− or the micro-USB's other outer pin.

Send me the readings if you're unsure and I'll point to the exact pad.
