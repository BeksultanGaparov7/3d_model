The OLED is the biggest consumer. Dark backgrounds with thin lines and a medium brightness can easily double the runtime. Another option is a sleep mode that wakes on a button press. In deep sleep the box draws about 0.05 mA, so a charge then lasts for months.

Wiring

Power: one USB-C port does everything

USB-C goes into the SuperMini. Its 5 V pin feeds the charger and the regulator. The battery joins through a diode, so it only takes over when USB is unplugged. While plugged in, the battery just charges, and the charger stops at 4.2 V. The battery is not discharged and recharged over and over.

Both buttons use the internal pull-ups. GPIO0 and GPIO1 can wake the chip from deep sleep. The pins the chip checks at boot (GPIO2, 8 and 9) are left free.
One small change on the charger board: TP4056 boards come set to 1 A, which is too much for a 400 mAh cell. Replace the resistor marked R3 or RPROG (usually “122” = 1.2 kΩ) with a 4.7 kΩ resistor. That gives about 250 mA, which is gentle and runs cool. The charger's own USB port stays unused.
